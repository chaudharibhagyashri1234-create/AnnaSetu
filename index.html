How AnnaSetu Works

A simple bridge between surplus food and people who need it.

1

Donor Posts Food

Restaurants, hostels and canteens post their available surplus food.

2

NGO Finds Food

NGOs can view available food, quantity and pickup information.

3

NGO Claims Food

An NGO claims the food and arranges pickup before expiry.

4

Food Is Collected

The food is collected and the saved meals are added to our impact.
